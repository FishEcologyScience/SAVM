The Submerged Aquatic Vegetation Model (SAVM) application is a decision-support tool designed to predict the presence, absence, and percent cover of submerged aquatic vegetation (SAV) in freshwater environments. This tool helps researchers, environmental managers, and conservation practitioners assess SAV distribution and support habitat management decisions.

What does SAVM do?
SAVM applies statistical models to predict SAV occurrence and cover based on environmental predictors, primarily water depth and wave fetch. The application offers three statistical approaches (Random Forest, GAM, and Linear Mixed Models) and includes optional post-hoc adjustments for water clarity, substrate limitations, and user-defined constraints.
