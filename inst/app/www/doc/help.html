SAVM Help

Getting Started
1 Data Input: Upload your spatial data (GeoPackage, GeoJSON, or Shapefile) containing location information and environmental variables.
2 Fetch Calculation: Calculate wave fetch distances for your study sites using the integrated fetch calculator with customizable parameters.
3 Model Application: Apply SAV prediction models by selecting your preferred statistical method, specifying predictor columns, and configuring post-hoc adjustments.
4 Results & Export: Review model predictions, visualize results on interactive maps, and export your data for further analysis.
Key Features
  • Multiple Statistical Methods: Choose between Random Forest, Generalized Additive Models (GAM), or Linear Mixed Models (LMM) for both presence/absence and cover predictions.
  • Flexible Column Mapping: Easily map your data columns to model requirements, with automatic detection of common naming conventions.
  • Post-hoc Refinements: Incorporate water clarity (Secchi depth), substrate limitations, and custom constraints to refine predictions.
  • Interactive Visualization: Explore predictions on interactive maps with tooltips showing detailed information for each point.
  • Integrated Fetch Calculator: Calculate wave fetch directly within the application using shoreline polygons and customizable parameters.
About the Models

The prediction models implemented in SAVM are based on extensive field surveys and statistical modeling of SAV distribution patterns in freshwater systems. The models use water depth and wave fetch as primary predictors, recognizing that these environmental factors strongly influence SAV colonization and growth.

Light Availability: When Secchi depth data is available, SAVM calculates the maximum depth of plant colonization (Vmax) using the Chambers and Kalff (1985) equation, accounting for light limitation effects on SAV distribution.
